Rules of engagement

  • Win a round by eliminating the other side, by the charge detonating (attack) or being defused, or by the clock running out (defence).
  • Kills, wins and losses pay. A losing streak pays more. Spend it on the Lancer AR, the Vigil DMR, armour, a defuse kit and grenades.
  • Heads take 4×. Spread grows with speed, airtime and spray length; the recoil pattern climbs, then wanders. Pull down.
  • Bots follow routes, hold angles, hear footsteps and gunfire, rotate on calls, smoke and flash their executes.
  • First to 7 rounds of 12. Sides swap after round 6.
